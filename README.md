Roots Signal

Roots Signal is a generative series exploring organic structure, growth, and latent tension through branching line systems.

The works resemble root networks, neural traces, or subterranean mappings — forms that emerge from simple rules but evolve into complex, layered structures. Each composition balances density and openness, control and drift, resulting in images that feel both natural and constructed.

The series consists of two palette variants, each containing 12 unique works:
• Light (linen / ash background with dark lines)
• Dark (near-black background with light lines)

Each artwork is rendered as a high-resolution PNG at 3000 × 4000 pixels and generated from a shared underlying system with internal variation.

The minted PNG is the canonical artwork. Each token includes access to supplementary bonus material in the form of a downloadable certificate. The certificate documents the work’s origin, structure, and edition context, and serves as an archival companion to the minted image.
